About

Asia Pacific Industry Group Co., Limited is a Chinese manufacturer of flexible packaging films, established in Hefei, Anhui Province. The company operates BOPP, BOPET and CPP film lines together with coating, slitting and converting capacity, and supplies converters, printers and brand owners in more than 80 countries. The film business sits alongside the group company www.apigcl.com, which serves the wider industrial and trading community.

01

Who we are

The company grew out of a packaging-materials trading operation and progressively moved upstream into film manufacturing. That history still shapes how it works: a great deal of the business is technical problem-solving for converters who need a specific grade running reliably on their own equipment, rather than a catalogue purchase. Production is organised around audited lines with in-line gauge and defect control, so a specification agreed once keeps holding over repeat orders.

02

Manufacturing and supply scope

We produce biaxially oriented polypropylene, biaxially oriented polyester and cast polypropylene, plus coating, metallising partners, printing and converting support. In-house capability covers corona treatment selection, coating, slitting, rewinding and finished-roll conversion. Typical output serves flexible packaging, labelling and thermal transfer ribbon converters, print-and-lamination houses and trading partners who supply them.

03

Quality, compliance and documentation

Quality management operates under an ISO-certified system covering incoming raw material, in-process gauging, defect inspection, finished-reel testing and traceability. Every reel carries a production identity that can be traced back to the line and the raw material lot. Food-contact declarations, REACH and RoHS statements and certificates of analysis are available per grade, and documentation is agreed before production rather than assembled afterwards.
